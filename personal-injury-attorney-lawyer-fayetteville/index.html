What the service covers and how a claim moves

A personal injury case can include reviewing the incident, gathering available evidence, communicating with insurance companies, and filing or litigating a claim when appropriate. The details depend on the type of incident and the information that can be obtained.

Key facts to keep in mind:
- What it covers: injury claims involving collisions, falls, dog attacks, other incidents, and wrongful death claims for families.
- Typical timing: there is no single timeline. The time involved can depend on the facts, the records available, communications with insurers, and whether litigation is appropriate.
- What affects cost: Jackson Injury Firm uses a contingency fee basis. The specific agreement explains the fee terms; there is no price stated here.
- What to bring up: describe what happened, any care or losses you have experienced, and any contact with an insurer. The firm can explain what records may be useful.

When comparing providers, ask who will answer your questions, how updates are handled, which parts of your claim the firm may address, and how the fee agreement works. Avoid choosing based on a promise of a particular result. “Evidence” means information that may help explain what happened, while “litigation” means taking a dispute through the court process.
